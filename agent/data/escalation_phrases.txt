# The keyword rule's phrases (issue #11). Grow this list whenever the eval or a call finds a miss.
#
# Written in normalised form: lower case, no punctuation, no apostrophes, and "cant" / "im" /
# "theres" / "wont" / "911" standing for every spelling of them (escalation.rs, `normalise`).
#
# [emergency]            the phrase anywhere, its words in order with at most 2 others between
# [emergency, whole]     the whole utterance, so "can you help me with the shopping" doesn't fire
# [asked for a person]   the phrase anywhere; every entry carries a verb of wanting, so "are you
#                        a real person?" doesn't fire (the LLM answers that one)
#
# "I need help" is left out on purpose: it is the natural answer to "do you need anything?",
# and that is a concern, not an emergency.
